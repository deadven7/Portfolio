SC 01 hello

Hi, I’m Amaan.

I’m a data scientist and engineer. I studied Computer Science at SRM and Statistics (Data Science) at Rutgers, and I’ve worked across machine-learning research, NLP and cloud engineering.

Computers have been around for as long as I can remember, and the curiosity never wore off. The question I bring to them has changed, though: less how does this work, more what is this data actually saying, and how do I show it?

SC 02 method

Patient by design.

Before I commit to a model, I want to know where it will break: what the data can and can’t support, which assumption is carrying the weight, and what the second-best explanation is.

It makes me a little slower on day one and a lot faster on day thirty. The work shows it: honest baselines, causal questions over convenient correlations, and results I can defend line by line.

SC 03 craft

Built to be seen.

Analysis nobody can follow doesn’t change anything. I put as much into the last mile (the chart, the interface, the explanation that survives a meeting) as into the model itself, because that’s where the work lands or doesn’t.

It’s why every project here comes with something you can actually play with.

SC 04 practice

Reps, then polish.

Most of what I’m good at came from repetition: small corrections, made thousands of times, until something holds. It’s the same loop as tuning a model, and I enjoy it more than I probably should.

SC 05 range

Comfortable in motion.

I’ve shipped as a team of one for a family export business, worked inside a global engineering team, and led the front end of a student newsroom’s cloud rebuild. I speak seven languages, adapt quickly, and like being handed the ambiguous part of a problem.
