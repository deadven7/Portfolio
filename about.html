LEVEL 1 · PLAYER ONEHi, I’m Amaan.

I’m a data scientist with a statistician’s suspicion of easy answers and an engineer’s need to ship. I studied Computer Science at SRM in Chennai, then Statistics (Data Science) at Rutgers in New Jersey.

I like problems that come with a story — the messier the data, the better the plot. My favourite part of the job is the moment a model stops being a black box and starts explaining itself.

LEVEL 2 · ORIGIN STORYOnes and zeros, since age two.

My first computer sessions were spent figuring out Windows and losing at Uno. At thirteen I picked a programming elective at school and met Java; competitive computing challenges, rudimentary games and the odd robotics project followed.

My first real work was building websites for my father’s pharmaceutical export business as a one-person team. Then, in the middle of the pandemic in 2020, I turned to data science — and it felt tailor-made. It still does.

It’s also why I built a checkers AI as one of my first projects — teaching a machine to look ahead is the closest thing to explaining chess to a friend.
